It started at fifteen, with my father’s phone and a keyboard I borrowed from a friend. I plugged it in and typed out the Python programs from our school textbooks, one line at a time, on a screen made for messages. Not many people around me shared this. Through school I had one or two friends who really understood it, and mostly I figured things out alone. At twenty-one, I finally built a proper setup at home. This is that desk. It took six years to get from a borrowed keyboard to it.
