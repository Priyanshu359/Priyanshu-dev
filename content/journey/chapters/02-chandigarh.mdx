Leaving home was sad, but every step away made me surer of the decision. Chandigarh University gave me the best days of my student life, and friends I’m still in touch with today. Getting through wasn’t easy. Even with a 50% merit scholarship, my family couldn’t cover the rest of the fees, so I took an education loan to finish my degree. My proudest moment was Anidex, a Flutter app I built that identifies animals from your camera, like a small Google Lens for animals. The first time it worked, I knew this was what I wanted to do.
