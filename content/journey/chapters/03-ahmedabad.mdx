I was the first in my close friend group to land an off-campus offer: a Flutter internship in Ahmedabad, where I ended up in the backend team. The city was lonely. I’m an introvert, and after office and on weekends there was no one to talk to. The stipend was small, so I took part-time shifts as a picker at quick-commerce dark stores to cover my needs and help at home. Then came my first real production bug. Real users were affected, and I fixed it. That was the moment I stopped feeling like a student and started feeling like a developer.
