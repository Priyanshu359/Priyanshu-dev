After the internship, I took a break to learn AI and build a career in it. I thought it would take a month or two to find a job. It took seven. An education loan to repay, pressure from family, pressure about my career, money running out. Every rejection asked the same question: was I even meant for coding? Should I try something else? I didn’t know what else, so I kept learning and kept building, one small thing at a time.
