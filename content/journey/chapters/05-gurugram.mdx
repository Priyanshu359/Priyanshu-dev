It happened twice. At my internship, I joined for Flutter and landed in the backend team. After the break, I joined as an AI engineer, and within a few months my backend and infrastructure work moved me into backend as an SDE-1. This time I didn’t resist it. I started enjoying it: the queues, the caches, the systems that have to hold up when thousands of people show up at once. The moment I knew I could do this was when Mentors Buddy went live, StudyIQ’s mentorship platform, whose backend I built end to end on my own. Today I own 20+ services, and I’m still learning every day.
